## MedAESQA data is off limits

The MedAESQA data, including `data/MedAESQA_methods_M1-M30.xlsx`, is ground truth.

- **Never modify it.** Do not edit, move, rename, overwrite, or delete it.
- **Never look at it.** Do not open, read, print, parse, summarize, or sample it, and do not write or run code that does. Viewing it could leak answers into our work, which would be cheating.

This applies to any file, copy, or derivative of the MedAESQA data, not just the path above. If a task seems to require this data, stop and ask first.
